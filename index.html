AgriMitra: Agricultural Decision-Support Platform

An AI-powered agricultural decision-support platform designed to help Indian farmers make data-driven decisions using machine learning, computer vision, satellite intelligence, and agentic workflows.

  • Problem: Smallholder farmers lack unified, localized guidance on crop selection, yield forecasts, and pest risks, often relying on guesswork or unverified inputs.
  • Yield & Crop Recommendation: Implemented Random Forest regression models to predict crop yield from soil parameters (NPK, moisture, pH) combined with historical weather data, alongside environmental crop recommendation pipelines.
  • Agentic AI Advisory: Designed an AI-driven workflow that synthesizes soil chemistry and weather forecasts to generate personalized fertilizer, irrigation, and soil management recommendations.
  • Vision & Remote Sensing: Deep-learning computer vision models with agricultural image datasets to identify crop diseases and pests, paired with Google Earth Engine vegetation indices (NDVI/NDRE) for field health monitoring.
  • Accessibility & Governance: Built with multilingual and voice-oriented interaction for users with limited digital literacy, plus an administrative dashboard for monitoring regional crop conditions.
  • Verifiable Result: Selected among Top 20 college-level software teams at VIT Chennai for Smart India Hackathon 2025 (Problem Statement ID 25044) and received campus incubation support through vNest.

SecureSwipe: Financial Transaction Fraud Classification

A machine-learning-based fraud detection project designed to identify suspicious financial transactions and assist risk analysis across digital payment channels.

  • Problem: Financial systems require rapid anomaly detection to catch fraudulent payment attempts without generating high false-positive friction for genuine users.
  • Model Training & SMOTE: Trained supervised classification models on transaction feature vectors, applying SMOTE to address heavy class imbalance between legitimate and fraudulent transactions.
  • Risk Scoring Pipeline: Structured an anomaly screening pipeline evaluating behavioral, location, and merchant discrepancies to assign risk scores for transaction verification.
  • Dual Dashboards: Built interactive interfaces comparing current fraud hotspot distributions against projected model-assisted risk mitigation scenarios across digital payment flows.
  • Verifiable Result: Won 1st Place at CodeZilla 2025 out of 90+ participating teams (450+ total participants).

SoberCare & PhaseSense: Recovery Stage Classification

An agentic AI-based recovery companion designed to analyze user-provided text and identify conversational patterns associated with different stages of recovery.

  • Problem: Individuals navigating recovery experience evolving psychological states that generic automated responses fail to interpret with adequate contextual nuance.
  • Semantic NLP Pipeline: Built PhaseSense NLP using BAAI/bge-small-en dense sentence embeddings, keyword signals, and ChromaDB vector similarity scoring.
  • Phase Classification: Maps user reflection inputs into 5 behavioral recovery stages (Denial/Minimization, Cognitive Negotiation, Active Craving, Heightened Vulnerability, Recovery/Stabilization) to inform supportive LLM response generation.
  • Empirical Decision Support Boundary: Engineered strictly as an empathetic decision-support companion and reflection aid — deliberately avoiding clinical diagnosis or medical treatment claims.
